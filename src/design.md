# Design System

A visual language modeled on 19th-century natural history illustration — the
hand-drawn taxonomic plates of Ernst Haeckel's *Kunstformen der Natur* and the
field journals of the Darwin era: aged paper, engraved rules, iron-gall ink,
and a touch of gilt.
